# HUD artwork

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool for the concept-inspired top HUD. All files are transparent PNGs and are rendered inside real HTML buttons so the existing labels, keyboard access and hit areas remain available.

Shared direction: premium 3D toy-like adventure-game icon, warm ivory and gold materials, cyan highlights, centered transparent square composition, no text, no watermark, no outer button, readable at small tablet HUD sizes.

- `sound.png`: ivory speaker horn with gold rim, cyan accent and two sound waves.
- `settings.png`: ivory and gold gear with a blue-teal gem center.
- `parent.png`: warm ivory adult support figure with teal clothing and a gold star badge.
- `leaderboard.png`: friendly golden trophy with a blue achievement ribbon for the upcoming class leaderboard.
- `challenge.png`: adventure scroll with compass seal and answer cards for the upcoming quiz challenge.
- `friends.png`: two friendly explorer badges sharing a small compass for classroom friends.
- `profile.png`: explorer identity badge with a portrait silhouette and star for Hồ sơ.
- `logout.png`: warm gold adventure doorway with an outgoing arrow for Đăng xuất.
- The passport badge reuses `public/art/dock/pet.png` so the fox identity remains consistent across the app.
